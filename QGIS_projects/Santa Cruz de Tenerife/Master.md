The current status of the urban planning for Santa Cruz de Tenerife is legally fragmented, as judicial rulings have systematically annulled the most recent comprehensive plans and their provisional adaptations. According to the "Planeamiento urbanístico de Santa Cruz de Tenerife - Conjunto de datos - SITCAN Open Data.pdf" file, the overarching General Plan (PGO) was struck down by the TSJC in sentence 116/2014, an annulment officially published in October 2020.   Following this collapse, the municipality reverted to the older 2005 framework (PGOU-05) and attempted to update it by approving a Provisional Municipal Ordinance for the regime of uses in May 2022. However, the TSJC subsequently suspended this 2022 provisional ordinance as well, via sentence 000070/2023. 

This urban planning instability creates significant legal uncertainty, particularly regarding building permits. Without a valid, up-to-date comprehensive plan, the legal framework for permitting becomes ambiguous. While some sources suggest a partial application of the 2005 framework or transitional regulations, the annulment of the 2022 ordinance specifically undermined the most recent attempt to provide clear rules for land use. This situation necessitates extreme caution for any development, as the validity of permits may be contested based on the conflicting legal statuses of the various planning instruments.
