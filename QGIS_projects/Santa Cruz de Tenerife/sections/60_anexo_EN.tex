\section{Cartography and Planning Samples}

The following section includes the series of official master plan maps of the General Urban Plan (PGO) in full-page landscape format, structured into the following structural and detailed zoning maps:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{PO-01}: Structural Planning Synthesis Model.
  \item \textbf{PO-02}: Land Classification and Categories.
  \item \textbf{PO-03}: Management Sectors and Planning Units.
  \item \textbf{PO-03B}: Sectors and Units (Complementary cartography).
  \item \textbf{PO-04}: Characteristic Global Land Uses.
\end{itemize}
This is followed by illustrative field cartography and sample official zoning certificates.
